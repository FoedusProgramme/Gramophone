Thank you for your interest in contributing to this project.

Please open a short, informal issue before starting substantial work on a pull request, so that scope, implementation details, etc can be discussed; not every feature is a great fit for our vision. Sometimes a PR you plan to send may change an area of code where a big refactor is happening at the moment, too, in which case we can warn you before you write all the code.

We, the maintainers, will do our best to accommodate pull requests that we weren't informed of in advance, but we may have to reject your contribution because we disagree with the initial idea, the implementation or even just the point in time you chose to open this PR. While it would be great to get every PR merged, we may sadly not have enough time to refactor your specific PR as well.

It's sad if we have to reject a well-intentioned PR for logistical reasons, and you may consider it a waste of your time too - this can be avoided if you discuss with us in advance. Thank you for your understanding!
